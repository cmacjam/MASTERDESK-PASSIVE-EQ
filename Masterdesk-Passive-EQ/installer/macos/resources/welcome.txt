Masterdesk Passive EQ by Basemental

A stereo, four-band parallel passive mastering equaliser with tube make-up gain,
stepped recallable controls, VU and harmonics metering.

Choose what to install on the next screens:
  - VST3 plug-in   -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  - Audio Unit     -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (Logic Pro, GarageBand)
  - Standalone app -> /Applications

After installing, rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic does this automatically on launch).
Inside the plug-in, press HELP ? for a guide to every control.
